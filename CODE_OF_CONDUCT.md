# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Assume good intent while reviewing the actual impact of a contribution. Disagreement about design is expected; harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- Giving actionable technical feedback
- Acknowledging trade-offs and uncertainty
- Respecting privacy and consent
- Helping contributors reproduce and verify problems
- Correcting mistakes without personal attacks

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through their GitHub profile. Do not include another person's private information in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the project or its community.
